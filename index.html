Internal validation, 200 patient-level bootstrap replicates內部驗證：200 次病人層級 bootstrap

Resampling was at the patient level, and the three removal passes were re-executed inside every replicate. Optimism is small, so overfitting does not explain the gap between the in-sample C-index and the landmark values.以病人為單位重抽樣，三道人工刪除規則在每次重抽樣內重跑。樂觀偏差很小，因此表觀 C-index 與 landmark 值之間的落差不是過度適配造成的。

!
The in-sample C-index of 0.846 is not a forecasting result.表觀 C-index 0.846 不是預測效能。 In a counting-process model the covariates are refreshed up to the moment of each event, so a rising white cell count or a falling eGFR in the days before death enters the comparison. Landmark analysis asks the question that matters clinically: freeze every covariate at one visit, then rank the patients for what happens afterwards.在 counting-process 模型中，共變項會更新到每個事件發生的當下，因此死亡前數日上升的白血球或驟降的 eGFR 都進入了比較。Landmark 分析問的才是臨床上要緊的問題：把所有變項凍結在某一次就診，再據此排序其後發生的事件。

Landmark discriminationLandmark 判別能力

At each landmark time every covariate is frozen at the value then in force, and the resulting risk score is used to rank patients for events occurring afterwards. Before about 15 months the baseline-only model ranks patients better; after that the time-varying model pulls ahead and the gap widens.在每個 landmark 時點把所有共變項凍結在當時的數值，再以所得風險分數排序其後發生的事件。約 15 個月之前，只用基線變項的模型排得更好；之後時變性模型才領先，且差距逐漸拉開。

!
Teaching and exploration only. This is not a prognostic calculator.僅供教學與探索，不是預後計算器。 It reports a hazard ratio against a reference profile at one instant — the quantity the model actually estimates. It deliberately does not report a survival probability, because that would require every covariate to stay fixed, which is the assumption this study shows to be false.本頁輸出的是「相對於參考病人、在某一瞬間」的風險比，也就是模型真正估計的量。本頁刻意不輸出存活機率，因為那需要假設所有共變項維持不變，而本研究正好顯示這個假設不成立。
